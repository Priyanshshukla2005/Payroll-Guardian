## Section 1: Statutory Coverage & Wage Limit Threshold

### 1. Monthly Wage Ceiling Limit
Under Rule 50 of the ESI (Central) Rules, 1950 (as amended), an employee whose **gross monthly wages (excluding overtime remuneration)** do not exceed **₹21,000 per month** is mandatorily coverable under the ESI Scheme.
- For employees with certified disabilities (under Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016), the statutory wage ceiling is **₹25,000 per month**.
- **Ineligible Employees**: Any employee whose regular gross monthly wages exceed ₹21,000.00 is strictly **exempt from ESI deduction**. Deducting ESI from an ineligible employee constitutes an erroneous payroll deduction.

### 2. Statutory Contribution Rates (Effective from 1st July 2019)
Vide Ministry of Labour and Employment Gazette Notification No. S.O. 2026(E) dated 13th June 2019, the reduced statutory contribution rates are:
- **Employee Share**: **0.75%** of gross monthly wages.
- **Employer Share**: **3.25%** of gross monthly wages.
- **Total Combined Contribution**: **4.00%** of gross monthly wages.

### 3. Computation Base for ESI
The computation of ESI must include:
- Basic salary
- Dearness Allowance (DA)
- House Rent Allowance (HRA)
- Conveyance, city compensatory, and special allowances
- Production/attendance incentives paid in the monthly pay cycle

*Exclusions*: Annual bonus, ex-gratia payments, gratuity, and retrenchment compensation are excluded from gross wages for ESI computation.

### 4. Mid-Period Wage Hike Continuity Rule
If the monthly gross wages of an employee exceed ₹21,000 after the start of a contribution period (April–September or October–March), the employee continues to be covered and deductions must continue until the end of that statutory contribution period.
