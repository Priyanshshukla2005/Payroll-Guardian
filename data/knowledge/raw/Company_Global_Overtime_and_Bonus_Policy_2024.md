## Section 1: Overtime Compensation Guidelines

### 1. Eligibility for Overtime
- Only non-exempt operational staff, technical support engineers, and customer operations specialists at `Intern`, `Junior`, or `Mid-level` designations are eligible for overtime compensation.
- Employees at `Senior`, `Manager`, or `Director` levels are exempt from overtime and receive fixed managerial salaries.

### 2. Overtime Rate Calculation Formula
Overtime hours approved in the biometric attendance system shall be compensated at **1.5x (one and a half times) the standard hourly basic wage rate**:
$$\text{Hourly Basic Rate} = \frac{\text{Basic Salary}}{\text{Working Days (26)} \times \text{Daily Hours (8)}}$$
$$\text{Overtime Payout} = \text{Overtime Hours} \times \text{Hourly Basic Rate} \times 1.50$$

### 3. Monthly Overtime Cap
- Maximum permissible overtime without special authorization is **40 hours per month**.
- Logging overtime in excess of **60 hours per month** triggers an automated operational compliance audit flag (`EXCESSIVE_OVERTIME`).

## Section 2: Annual Performance & Festive Bonus Policy

### 1. Festive Diwali Bonus (October Cycle)
All active employees completed probation as of 30th September are eligible for a discretionary festive Diwali bonus ranging from ₹15,000 to ₹60,000 based on employee band.

### 2. Annual Performance Appraisal Bonus (March Cycle)
Performance bonuses disbursed during the March annual appraisal cycle range from 8% to 25% of annual basic salary based on verified performance appraisal rating (Exceeds Expectations / Outstanding).

### 3. Out-of-Cycle Discretionary Bonuses
Any ad-hoc bonus exceeding ₹100,000 outside the standard March/October appraisal cycles requires dual authorization from the Chief Financial Officer and VP of People Operations.
