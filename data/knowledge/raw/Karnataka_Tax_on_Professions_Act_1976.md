## Schedule: Rates of Tax on Salaried Persons in Karnataka State

### 1. Statutory Deduction Slabs
Under Section 3 and the Schedule to the Karnataka Tax on Professions, Trades, Callings and Employments Act, 1976 (as amended by Karnataka Act No. 14 of 2023):

| Monthly Gross Salary Range | Statutory Monthly Tax Payable |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Less than ₹15,000.00** | **₹0.00 (Nil - Exempt)** |
| **₹15,000.00 and Above** | **₹200.00 per month** |

### 2. Annual Limit and Exemption Threshold
- The exemption threshold for salaried employees in Karnataka was officially raised from ₹10,000 to **₹15,000 per month** effective 1st April 2023.
- For all employees with gross earnings $\ge ₹15,000$, a uniform flat deduction of **₹200.00 per month** (₹2,400.00 annually) is statutory mandated.
- Any deduction exceeding ₹200/month or deducting Professional Tax from an employee earning $< ₹15,000$ in Karnataka is non-compliant.

### 3. Senior Citizen Exemption
Employees aged 60 years and above are fully exempt from Professional Tax in the state of Karnataka.
