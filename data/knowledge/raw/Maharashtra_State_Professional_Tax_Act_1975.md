## Schedule I: Schedule of Professional Tax Deduction Slabs for Salary Earners

### 1. Monthly Deduction Slabs for Salaried Employees in Maharashtra
Under Section 3 and Schedule I of the Maharashtra State Tax on Professions Act, 1975 (as amended by Maharashtra Finance Act, 2023):

| Monthly Gross Salary Range | Monthly Tax for Male Employees | Monthly Tax for Female Employees |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Up to ₹7,500.00** | **₹0.00 (Nil)** | **₹0.00 (Nil)** |
| **₹7,501.00 to ₹10,000.00** | **₹175.00** | **₹0.00 (Nil)** |
| **₹10,001.00 to ₹25,000.00** | **₹200.00** *(₹300 in Feb)* | **₹0.00 (Nil - Exempt)** |
| **Above ₹25,000.00** | **₹200.00** *(₹300 in Feb)* | **₹200.00** *(₹300 in Feb)* |

### 2. February Surcharge Adjustment Rule
To ensure the annual total deduction equals exactly the statutory maximum cap of **₹2,500.00 per annum**:
- For months March to January (11 months): **₹200.00 per month** ($11 \times 200 = ₹2,200$)
- For the month of February (1 month): **₹300.00**
- Total Annual Tax = $2,200 + 300 = \text{₹2,500.00}$.

### 3. Exemption for Female Employees
Vide 2023 amendment, women earning monthly gross salary up to **₹25,000.00** are completely exempt from Professional Tax in Maharashtra state.
