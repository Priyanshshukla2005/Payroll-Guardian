## Section 192: Deduction of Tax at Source on Salaries

### 1. Statutory Employer Obligation
Any person responsible for paying any income chargeable under the head "Salaries" shall, at the time of payment, deduct income-tax on the amount payable at the **average rate of income-tax computed on the basis of the rates in force for the financial year** in which the payment is made, on the estimated income of the assessee under this head for that financial year.

### 2. Method of Monthly TDS Computation
The employer must:
1. Estimate the employee's total gross annual salary earnings across the entire 12-month financial year (April to March), including basic, allowances, regular bonuses, and overtime projections.
2. Deduct statutory standard deduction of **₹50,000** (under Section 16(ia)).
3. Apply declared tax regime (Default New Tax Regime under Section 115BAC or Old Tax Regime).
4. Calculate net total annual tax liability including 4% Health & Education Cess.
5. Divide net annual tax liability equally across the remaining payroll months of the financial year to determine the monthly TDS deduction amount.

### 3. Adjustment for Bonuses and Increment Arrears
When an employee receives a one-time performance bonus or salary revision increment, the employer is statutory required to recalculate the estimated annual taxable income and adjust the monthly TDS deduction proportionately for the remaining months of the financial year to prevent year-end tax deficit.

### 4. Non-Deduction and Default Penalties
Under Section 201(1A), failure to deduct or deposit TDS attracts mandatory interest at **1.5% per month** from the date on which tax was deductible to the date of actual payment into the government treasury.
