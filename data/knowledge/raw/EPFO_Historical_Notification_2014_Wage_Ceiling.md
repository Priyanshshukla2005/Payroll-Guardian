## Historical Notification: Wage Ceiling Enhancement from ₹6,500 to ₹15,000

### 1. Historical Background
Vide Ministry of Labour and Employment Notification G.S.R. 609(E) dated 22nd August 2014, effective from 1st September 2014:
- The statutory wage ceiling for mandatory membership under the Employees' Provident Funds Scheme was enhanced from the prior limit of **₹6,500 per month** to **₹15,000 per month**.
- Employers were instructed to enroll all employees drawing basic wages up to ₹15,000 per month into EPF Scheme.

### 2. Historical Validity
This notification governs historical wage eligibility for payroll periods between September 2014 and December 2023. For payroll periods in 2024 and beyond, the updated consolidated provisions under `EPFO_ACT_1952` apply.
